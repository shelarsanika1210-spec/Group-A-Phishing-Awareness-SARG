🚩 How to Identify Phishing?

  • Check Sender: Email was not from official domain
  • Urgent Language: "Your account will be locked"
  • Fake URL: Hover over links to see real URL
  • Login via Email: Companies never ask password via email link

🛡️ How to Stay Safe?

  • Never click unknown links
  • Always type official website directly
  • Enable 2-Factor Authentication
  • Report phishing to your IT team
